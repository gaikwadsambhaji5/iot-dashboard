Configuration / Local device
System settings

Settings are saved in this browser only. Changing the expected sample interval affects the estimated data coverage and gap analysis; it does not change how often the ESP32 transmits. The dashboard cannot distinguish a power cut from Wi-Fi loss without additional hardware or backend status data.
